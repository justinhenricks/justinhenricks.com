# Hello world

Welcome to my corner of the internet! This is my very first post, and I’m excited to kick things off by introducing myself. My name is Justin Henricks, and I’m writing to you from the charming city of Troy, NY. As of tomorrow, I’ll be hitting the big 3-2—time really flies when you’re having fun!

So, who am I, and what’s this blog all about? I’m a musician and a software developer with a passion for both technology and creativity. By day, I’m deeply immersed in the world of code as a developer at Shopify. For those who might not be familiar, Shopify is a leading e-commerce platform that empowers entrepreneurs and businesses to create and manage their online stores. It’s a dynamic and innovative environment, and I’m thrilled to be part of a team that’s constantly pushing boundaries and transforming the way people shop online.

When I’m not coding away, you’ll likely find me with a guitar in hand or exploring new destinations. Music is my other great passion. Whether it’s strumming chords, writing songs, or just enjoying a good jam session, music has always been a vital part of my life. It’s a way for me to express myself and unwind, and it often fuels my creativity in both my personal and professional life.

Traveling is another pursuit that excites me. There’s something incredibly enriching about experiencing new cultures, meeting people from different backgrounds, and exploring unfamiliar landscapes. It broadens my perspective and often inspires me in ways I can’t quite put into words. From bustling cities to serene natural vistas, each journey offers a new story and fresh ideas to reflect upon.

Through this blog, I hope to share my experiences and insights—both as a developer at Shopify and as someone who finds joy in music and travel. I’ll be diving into topics related to software development, offering glimpses into the world of Shopify, and sharing some of my adventures and musical musings along the way.

I’m excited to embark on this blogging journey and invite you to join me as I explore, create, and share. Here’s to new beginnings and the stories we’ll uncover together!

Stay tuned for more posts, and feel free to connect with me—whether you’re into tech, music, travel, or just curious about what’s next.

Thanks for stopping by!
